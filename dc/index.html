14 — Methodology

How I Built This

Data pipeline: Crash records queried from DCGIS ArcGIS REST API (MapServer layer 24), paginated at 1,000 records per call. 18,175 records for 2025. Coordinates are native WGS84.
Ward assignment: DC data includes a WARD field directly (Ward 1 through Ward 8). Records without a ward are excluded. Records without usable coordinates are kept in every total and left off the map. Times are converted from UTC to Eastern time; a report stored at exactly midnight has no recorded time and is left out of the hourly charts.
Behavioral flags: DRIVERSIMPAIRED and SPEEDING_INVOLVED are pre-computed integer fields. Pedestrian and cyclist involvement from TOTAL_PEDESTRIANS and TOTAL_BICYCLES counts. Hit-and-run data is not available in this dataset. Injury severity uses KABCO breakdown by role (driver, pedestrian, bicyclist, passenger).

Data: DC Open Data · Vision Zero DC · DDOT
