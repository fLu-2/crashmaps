About this map and your privacy

Privacy: This page has no backend, no analytics, and no cookies, and nothing you type is stored by crashmaps. Loading it fetches fonts from Google Fonts, scripts from cdnjs (Cloudflare) and map tiles from CARTO, and each of those services sees your IP address. An address you search is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, and commute start and end points are sent to the public OSRM routing server. Those requests are subject to each service’s privacy policy.

12 — Sources

Where This Data Comes From

CHP CCRS
California Highway Patrol publishes all crash records through the Crash Records System (CCRS) on data.ca.gov. 14,281 crashes in Santa Clara County in 2025, with severity and behavioral flags from the Parties detail table.

CCRS Dataset on data.ca.gov
13 — Methodology

How I Built This

Raw CCRS crash records are queried from the CKAN DataStore API, filtered to County Code 43 (Santa Clara). Party-level data is joined by Collision ID to extract DUI/impairment status and speed limits. Crashes that cannot be geocoded are kept in every total and left off the map. 14,281 records processed for 2025.

Data: CHP CCRS
