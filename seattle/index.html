14 — Methodology

How I Built This

Raw SDOT collision records are queried from the ArcGIS FeatureServer for each year. Coordinates are requested in WGS84 (outSR=4326). Records without valid coordinates are kept in every total under “Location not recorded” and left off the map. Neighborhoods are assigned via coordinate bounding boxes. Crash types are derived from SDOT collision descriptions. 5,764 records processed for 2025. In October 2026 SDOT reclassified motorized-scooter riders struck by vehicles from cyclists to pedestrians in its collision data, so pedestrian counts here include them in every year shown.
Hotspots: A hotspot is every crash within roughly a 200-metre area. 16 of the 65 hotspots on this page come from records that give a street with no cross street. Those are grouped by 200-metre square and labelled “Near [street] & [nearest cross street]”, with the cross street taken from OpenStreetMap. The count covers every crash in the square, including crashes on the other streets inside it. Squares next to each other are listed separately.
